## Narrative: A home that holds its place in the city

On Ambli-Bopal Road, Parijaat Eclat is not trying to be a skyline gesture first. It is a place to live. Forty-eight homes, stacked through twenty-three floors, give the project a scarcity that residents can feel: fewer doors on the corridor, more of the building belonging to the people who live there.

The apartments and penthouses are planned as luxury 4 BHK homes. That is the client value, stated plainly. A four-bedroom plan is a family plan — rooms that can hold parents, children, guests, and the quieter work of daily life — rather than a show flat stretched across a typical floor.

The facade is exposed concrete. In Ahmedabad that material is not a fashion. It is a way of making a high-rise feel solid in the hand and honest in the light. For the people who live here, the building does not hide behind a borrowed skin. It keeps a calm face to the road and a durable one to the weather.

The project sheet records integration with the Privilon commercial hub. That matters at the scale of a day. Home and work sit in the same stretch of Ambli-Bopal Road, so the journey between them can stay short, and the residential address does not have to turn its back on the life of the corridor. This is a recorded relationship between two named projects. It is not a merged credit.

This is Vitan Architects’ work, led by Ar. Jagrut Patel. The firm’s measure is simple: adding life, every square foot — not by claiming more building than the facts allow, but by making the homes that exist feel complete.
